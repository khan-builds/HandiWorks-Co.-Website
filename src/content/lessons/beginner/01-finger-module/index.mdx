This lesson has three parts. First you draw the finger. Then you wire it. Then you program it.

The finished finger is about 11.5 inches long (291 mm).

<Callout type="note" title="New words">
A **joint** is a round hole with a pin through it. A **link** is a bar that swings on a joint. The **motor** turns to move the links. **CAD** is drawing a part in 3D on a computer.
</Callout>

## What you will have

A finger model that matches the finished example, a copy of it with one change of your own, and a Mega that closes the finger on one press of the button and opens it on the next.
