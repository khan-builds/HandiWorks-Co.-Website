01 / A helping hand

Big ideas start with a little hands-on thinking.

HelpingHand brings mechanics, electronics, and programming together in one modular robotics project. A tangible way for students to explore how engineering turns an idea into movement.

02 / Meet the mechanism

Engineered for hands-on learning.

01 / Mechanics

Small links. Coordinated movement.

The proposed fingers use a scissor mechanism. Explore how connected links translate motion through each finger.

02 / Electronics

A little power. A lot to explore.

An Arduino Mega, six positional servos, and a three-axis gyroscope and accelerometer form the planned electronic system.

03 / Programming

From your code to its fingertips.

Open and close inputs guide the hand toward its target position. The proposed control logic includes fault checks and LED status feedback.

03 / Learning through making

Learn through experimentation.

Designed for students learning engineering and robotics. Follow the connections from physical parts to circuits to the code that brings them together.

  1. 01

    Discover the parts.

    Get to know the fingers, palm, base, and mechanical linkages.

  2. 02

    Connect the ideas.

    Explore how the controller, power supply, sensors, and servos work together.

  3. 03

    Make your next move.

    Investigate the control logic. Test, observe, and use what you learn to improve the design.
