HelpingHand

02 / Meet the mechanism

Engineered for hands-on learning.

01 / Mechanics

Small links. Coordinated movement.

The proposed fingers use a scissor mechanism. Explore how connected links translate motion through each finger.

02 / Electronics

A little power. A lot to explore.

An Arduino Mega, six positional servos, and a three-axis gyroscope and accelerometer form the planned electronic system.

03 / Programming

From your code to its fingertips.

Open and close inputs guide the hand toward its target position. The proposed control logic includes fault checks and LED status feedback.
