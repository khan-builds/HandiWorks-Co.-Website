## How a finger moves
A linkage connects rigid parts at pivot points. Moving one link can change the position of the others. The proposed lesson explores two coupled four-bar linkages. Compare the joints with the knuckles of your own finger.

## Safety first
<aside className="callout safety">Disconnect power before adjusting parts. Keep fingers clear of pinch points. Use tools and hot glue with your teacher’s supervision.</aside>

## Assemble the finger
The following planning sequence needs the hardware team's photos and exact fastener specifications before classroom use.

1. Identify the kit parts against the final bill of materials.
2. Inspect printed parts for damage or obstructed holes.
3. Identify the proximal and distal finger sections.
4. Locate the linkage pivot positions in the approved drawing.
5. Fit the linkage hardware specified by the hardware team.
6. Fit the drive housing and approved drive components.
7. Check that fasteners retain the joints without clamping them rigidly.
8. Move the mechanism gently by hand with power disconnected.
9. Stop and correct any rubbing or binding.
10. Ask your teacher to review the assembly before connecting power.

## Program it
This pseudocode describes the intended behavior; it is not an uploadable sketch.

```text
start with an open target
read the debounced button
when a new press is detected:
    switch between open and closed targets
if a fault is detected:
    stop the motor
otherwise:
    move toward the target within validated limits
```

The tested Arduino download and Wokwi project are pending hardware validation.

## Test your finger
With the approved wiring and sketch installed, check full travel, smooth movement, and ten consecutive open/close cycles. Disconnect power immediately if the mechanism binds, overheats, or moves unexpectedly.

## Check your understanding
1. What is the purpose of a pivot joint?
2. Why should you check free movement before powering the motor?
3. What does button debouncing prevent?
4. Why must the motor stop when a fault occurs?
5. What would you change to request a halfway position?

<TeacherOnly title="Answer key and assessment">
A pivot allows relative rotation. A free-movement check catches binding. Debouncing prevents multiple events from one press. Fault handling limits unsafe motion. A halfway target must remain inside validated travel limits. Assess explanation, careful assembly, safe testing, and the student's interpretation of results.
</TeacherOnly>
